Face tracking

Turn off ActiveTrack on the camera first, so the two don't fight.

Moving the camera yourself (d-pad, keys, presets, Level, Hold) pauses tracking. Press T or click to resume.

Gimbal

While Holding, the camera stays where you point it. Release hands framing back to the camera's follow / tracking.

Front / selfie is switched on the camera itself. Moves stay on that side. Keep the camera body fairly upright: if it leans far forward, a move can flip selfie to front.
